# Privacy Policy for C64U Slim-Viewer

Last updated November 16, 2026

**Data controller:** Christoph Schäfer (Grütze-Software), schaefer.christoph@posteo.de

## Video, audio, and networking

C64U Slim-Viewer receives video and optional audio from a Commodore 64 Ultimate or Ultimate 64 over UDP on your local network. The app displays your computer's local IP address to help you set up streaming. It processes the incoming streams on your device for live playback. The app does not record the streams or send them to the developer or external servers.

## Settings and Microsoft Store

The app saves your selected UDP ports and your audio and CRT preferences in a local settings file. The file remains on your device until you delete it or save new settings.

The app requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics, or telemetry. The Microsoft Store version does not check GitHub for updates. Microsoft's handling of data through the Store is described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

## External links and contact

When you follow a link to GitHub or Ko-fi from the app, the website opens in your browser. Each provider's privacy policy applies to its website.
